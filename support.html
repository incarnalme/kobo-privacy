Support

Kobo: Memory & Strategy Game · for Android and iPhone

Contact us

Email greymatterapplications@gmail.com with any question, problem or idea. Please say which phone you use (Android or iPhone). We usually reply within two working days.

Learning to play

Tap Tutorial at the top of the home screen for a guided 2-minute practice round. The full rules are under Rules in the bottom bar.

Your name, scores and settings

They are saved on your phone only. Tap your name on the home screen to change it. Deleting the app also deletes your scores.

Playing with friends

iPhone: tap Friends → Create a room and send the 4-letter code to your friends with Share invite. They tap Friends → Join a room and type the code. You need an internet connection, and the host should keep Kobo open: if the host leaves or closes the app, the room closes. If a player drops out during a game, a computer player takes their seat.

Android: friend rooms are coming in a future update.

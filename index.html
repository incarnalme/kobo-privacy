Privacy policy

Kobo: Memory & Strategy Game (Android and iPhone app) · Effective 20 November 2026 (friend rooms on Android and iPhone)

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the game "Kobo: Memory & Strategy Game" (app ID com.greymatterapps.kobo), published by GreyMatterApps on Google Play and the Apple App Store. For any question about privacy, email greymatterapplications@gmail.com.

Summary

We do not sell or share personal data, and there is no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service. When you play against computer opponents, nothing leaves your phone. When you play with friends in a room, the small amount of information described under "Playing with friends" passes through our game server only while the room is open.

Data stored on your phone only

To work, the app saves the following on your phone: the player name and emblem you choose, your settings (sound, table style, cards per hand, where you put the button dock), whether you have finished the tutorial plus the history of your last 100 games (date, number of players, your result and points). This information stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this saved information in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is switched on, iOS may include this saved information in your iCloud backup. That backup is managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

You can delete all of this information at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling (deleting) the app.

Playing with friends

Friend rooms run on Google Firebase. The rooms themselves are stored in the Firebase Realtime Database in Belgium (European Union). Firebase Authentication, which issues the random user ID below, is run by Google and may process that ID outside the UK and EU. Like any internet service, Firebase also receives your phone's IP address while it is connected. When you create or join a room:

  • The app signs you in anonymously. Firebase gives your copy of the app a random user ID. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Google Account or Apple Account, and we never ask for any of these. The ID stays on your phone and is reused for later rooms until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Your player name and emblem are shared with the other players in the room so they can see who is playing.
  • The moves of the game pass through the server so every player's screen stays up to date. Each player is only sent their own view of the cards.

While a game is on, the room also keeps a copy of the game so another player can take over as host if the host drops out. Room information is deleted when the last player leaves the room. If a room is closed without anyone leaving it (for example, if every phone loses its connection at once), its information can stay on the server until we delete it; you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing us with the room code. We do not keep a copy, and we do not use it for anything except running the game. Firebase processes it on our behalf under Google's terms (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). There is no chat in the app.

Deleting your data

This applies to Kobo: Memory & Strategy Game by GreyMatterApps.

  • Information on your phone (player name, emblem, settings and game history): clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. This deletes it straight away and also removes the random friend-room ID from your phone.
  • Friend-room information on our server (the random ID, player name, emblem and game moves of a room): this is deleted when the last player leaves the room. To ask us to delete anything that is left, email greymatterapplications@gmail.com with the subject "Kobo data deletion" and, if you know it, the room code. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

We do not keep any of this information after it is deleted, and there is no account to close.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Future features

We plan to add player accounts, usernames, a friends list and profile photos. Before any of these features is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is collected, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

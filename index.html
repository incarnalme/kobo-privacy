Privacy policy

Kobo: Memory & Strategy Game (Android and iPhone app) · Effective 20 November 2026 (friend rooms added)

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the game "Kobo: Memory & Strategy Game" (app ID com.greymatterapps.kobo), published by GreyMatterApps on Google Play and the Apple App Store. For any question about privacy, email greymatterapplications@gmail.com.

Summary

We do not sell or share personal data, and there is no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service. When you play against computer opponents, nothing leaves your phone. When you play with friends in a room (iPhone app), the small amount of information described under "Playing with friends" passes through our game server only while the room is open.

Data stored on your phone only

To work, the app saves the following on your phone: the player name and emblem you choose, your settings (sound, table style, cards per hand, where you put the button dock), whether you have finished the tutorial plus the history of your last 100 games (date, number of players, your result and points). This information stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this saved information in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is switched on, iOS may include this saved information in your iCloud backup. That backup is managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

You can delete all of this information at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling (deleting) the app.

Playing with friends (iPhone app)

Friend rooms run on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and the Firebase Realtime Database, hosted in the European Union). When you create or join a room:

  • The app signs you in anonymously. Firebase gives your copy of the app a random user ID. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple Account, and we never ask for any of these.
  • Your player name and emblem are shared with the other players in the room so they can see who is playing.
  • The moves of the game pass through the server so every player's screen stays up to date. Each player is only sent their own view of the cards.

This room information is deleted automatically when the room's host leaves or closes the app. We do not keep a copy, and we do not use it for anything except running the game. Firebase processes it on our behalf under Google's terms (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). There is no chat in the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Future features

We plan to add online play on Android, player accounts, usernames, a friends list and profile photos. Before any of these features is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is collected, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

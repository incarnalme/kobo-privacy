Privacy policy

Kobo: Memory & Strategy Cards (Android app) · Effective 11 November 2026

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the game "Kobo: Memory & Strategy Cards" (package com.greymatterapps.kobo), published on Google Play by GreyMatterApps. For any question about privacy, email greymatterapplications@gmail.com.

Summary

This version of the app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There is no account or sign-in. There is no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service. The app does not send any information to us or to any server. You play against computer opponents on your own phone.

Data stored on your phone only

To work, the app saves the following on your phone: the player name and emblem you choose, your settings (sound, table style, cards per hand, where you put the button dock), whether you have finished the tutorial plus the history of your last 100 games (date, number of players, your result and points). This information stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include this saved information in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

You can delete all of this information at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Future features

We plan to add online play with friends, player accounts, usernames, a friends list and profile photos. Before any of these features is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is collected, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
